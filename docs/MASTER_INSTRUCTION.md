**3. TECHNICAL ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK**

For technical analysis, examine the following indicators and price
behaviour.

**A. EMA ANALYSIS**

Mandatory EMA indicators:

- EMA 21.

- EMA 63.

- EMA 200.

Analyze on daily candles and weekly charts wherever appropriate.

Evaluate:

1.  Current price relative to all three EMAs.

2.  EMA 21 above or below EMA 63.

3.  EMA 63 above or below EMA 200.

4.  Whether the EMAs are rising, falling or flattening.

5.  Bullish and bearish crossovers.

6.  Price reclaiming important EMAs.

7.  Price rejecting EMAs as resistance.

8.  Distance between current price and EMAs.

9.  Whether the stock is overextended.

Interpret the overall EMA structure.

Do not classify a stock as bullish merely because its price is above EMA
21.

**B. RSI ANALYSIS**

Use RSI 14 as the default.

Analyze:

- RSI above 70: Potential overbought conditions.

- RSI between 50 and 70: Positive momentum, subject to trend.

- RSI between 30 and 50: Weak momentum.

- RSI below 30: Potential oversold conditions.

Check:

1.  RSI trend and direction.

2.  RSI crossing above or below 50.

3.  Bullish divergence.

4.  Bearish divergence.

5.  Failure swings.

6.  Momentum confirmation.

RSI above 70 does not automatically mean SELL, and RSI below 30 does not
automatically mean BUY.

Consider the prevailing trend and price structure.

**C. CANDLESTICK ANALYSIS**

Analyze the latest 60 trading sessions wherever data is available.

Identify:

- Bullish engulfing.

- Bearish engulfing.

- Hammer.

- Shooting star.

- Morning star.

- Evening star.

- Doji.

- Inside bar.

- Strong breakout candles.

- Rejection candles.

- Long upper and lower wicks.

Explain the significance of the candle in its price context.

Do not identify a candlestick pattern without sufficient price data.

**D. VOLUME ANALYSIS**

Evaluate:

- Current volume versus 20-day average volume.

- Volume expansion during upward movements.

- Volume expansion during downward movements.

- Breakout volume confirmation.

- Distribution patterns.

- Accumulation patterns.

- Delivery volume trends.

Volume must confirm price action wherever possible.

**E. DELIVERY PERCENTAGE**

Analyze delivery percentage using historical daily data.

Check:

- Current delivery percentage.

- 5-day average.

- 10-day average.

- 20-day average.

- Changes in delivery percentage.

- Delivery percentage during price increases.

- Delivery percentage during price declines.

Identify possible accumulation or distribution patterns.

High delivery percentage alone is not proof of institutional
accumulation.

**F. SUPPORT AND RESISTANCE**

Identify:

- Immediate support.

- Strong support.

- Immediate resistance.

- Major resistance.

- Previous swing highs and lows.

- Consolidation zones.

- Breakout levels.

- Breakdown levels.

Provide actual price levels supported by historical chart data.

Do not invent support or resistance prices.

**G. GAP-UP / GAP-DOWN ANALYSIS**

Examine:

- Previous closing price.

- Opening gap.

- Gap percentage.

- Gap filling.

- Gap support and resistance.

- Volume confirmation.

For positional trade entries, identify whether chasing a sharp gap-up
creates an unfavourable risk-reward situation.

Do not automatically reject every gap-up. Evaluate the reason and
strength of the breakout.

**H. TREND STRUCTURE**

Identify:

- Higher Highs and Higher Lows.

- Lower Highs and Lower Lows.

- Sideways consolidation.

- Trend reversal.

- Breakout.

- Breakdown.

- Bullish and bearish divergence.

**I. POSITIONAL TRADING DECISION FRAMEWORK**
